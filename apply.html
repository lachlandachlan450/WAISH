Talks and socials are open to everyone — just turn up. The technical bootcamp has a short application so we can keep cohorts small and well matched.

The bootcamp

A termly programme following the ARENA curriculum: PyTorch fundamentals, transformer architecture, mechanistic interpretability, and reinforcement learning. Sessions run weekly, mixing guided exercises with pair programming. Expect to commit a few hours a week outside sessions.

You need comfort with programming (any language) and first-year level maths. No machine learning background required.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the form

    Tell us your course, year, programming background, and why you're interested. It takes about ten minutes — no CV needed.

  2. Short chat

    We'll invite you to an informal fifteen-minute conversation, mostly to gauge fit and answer your questions.

  3. Join the cohort

    Offers go out within a week of the deadline. Sessions start in week two of term.
