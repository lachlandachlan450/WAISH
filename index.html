Making advanced AI go well, from Warwick

The Warwick AI Safety Hub is a student community working on the technical and strategic problems of making powerful AI systems safe. We run reading groups, a hands-on upskilling bootcamp, and research projects.

What we do

Technical bootcamp

A termly upskilling programme built on the ARENA curriculum: transformer internals, mechanistic interpretability, RL and evals, taught through weekly coding sessions.

Research projects

Small teams work on open problems and hackathon projects, with the aim of producing publishable write-ups and building genuine research experience.

Talks and socials

Guest speakers from safety labs and academia, plus regular socials — a low-commitment way to meet the community.

Why AI safety

AI capabilities are advancing quickly, and the field of people working to make these systems safe, interpretable and controllable is still small. Students with strong mathematical and technical backgrounds are unusually well placed to contribute — and Warwick has plenty of them.

You don't need prior machine learning experience to get involved. The bootcamp starts from foundations, and our talks and socials are open to anyone curious about the problems.
